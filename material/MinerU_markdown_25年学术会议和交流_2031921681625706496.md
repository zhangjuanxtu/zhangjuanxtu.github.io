1. 2025 年 1⽉13-15日，数值代数与快速算法中青年研讨会Title: Numerical method and preconditioning of quasiperiodicSchrödinger eigenproblems

Abstract: In quantum systems, the localization of waves is closely related to the electrical structure’s disorder. The quasiperiodic Schrödinger eigenproblem is a typical mathematical model that describes this phenomenon. However, due to quasiperiodic potentials are space- filling order without decay or translation invariance, the condition numbers of quasiperiodic Schrödinger operators very poor. In this report, I will introduce a new algorithm, the irrational-window-filtered projection method (IWFPM), which uses the concentrated distribution feature of spectral points to further improve the projection method. Moreover, we also designed a preconditioner that perfectly matches quasiperiodic Schrödinger operators, drastically reducing each condition number from tens of thousands to single digits. We apply IWFPM to one-, two- and three-dimensional quasiperiodic Schrödinger eigenproblems to demonstrate the accuracy and efficiency of our proposed method. Especially when solving localized quantum states, IWFPM shows significant advantages over the projection method.

# 3. 2025 年 4⽉11-13 日，“数值代数的机遇与挑战”学术研讨会，山东潍坊

Title: Parameter selection and preconditioning of iterative methods for linear systems 

Abstract: The iterative solution of linear systems—including those derived from discretized differential equations, time-dependent linear systems, and linear eigenvalue problems—remains a longstanding challenge due to difficulties in parameter selection and the reduction of condition numbers. In this talk, I will introduce a data-driven approach for predicting optimal iterative parameters: multi-task kernel learning Gaussian process regression (GPR) method. 

Building on this, develop the generalized alternating direction implicit (GADI) framework with optimal parameters, successfully integrating it as a smoother in algebraic multigrid methods to solve linear systems. We have successfully applied GPR to (time-dependent) linear algebraic systems (convection-diffusion equations, Helmholtz equations) and linear matrix equations (Sylvester equations). When the system size exceeds hundreds of thousands, the acceleration ratio of the GADI framework can reach hundreds to thousands of times. Moreover, for solving quasiperiodic Schrödinger eigenproblems—where potentials exhibit space-filling structures without decay or translation invariance—we propose the irrational-window-filtered projection method (IWFPM). We designed a preconditioner that perfectly matches quasiperiodic Schrödinger operators, drastically reducing each condition number from tens of thousands to single digits. We apply IWFPM to one-, two- and three-dimensional quasiperiodic Schrödinger eigenproblems to demonstrate the accuracy and efficiency of our proposed method.

8. 2025 年 6⽉23-25⽇，2025 数值代数及其应用研讨会，上海交通大学

Title: Numerical Method and Preconditioning of Quasiperiodic Elliptic Equations 

Abstract: In this study, our primary objective is to address the challenge 

of efficiently solving elliptic equations with quasiperiodic coefficients from a numerical algebra perspective. To achieve accurate and efficient computations, we introduce the projection method, which enables the embedding of quasiperiodic systems into higher-dimensional periodic systems. To further enhance computational efficiency and stability, we propose a compressed storage strategy for the stiffness matrix, reducing memory requirements while maintaining accuracy. Most importantly, we design and implement a diagonal preconditioner to efficiently solve the resulting high-dimensional linear system by significantly reducing the condition number of the stiffness matrix. These combined techniques contribute significantly to the computational effectiveness of our proposed approach. We demonstrate the effectiveness and accuracy of our approach through a series of numerical examples and apply our method to achieve highly accurate computation of the homogenized coefficients for a quasiperiodic multiscale elliptic equation.

# 14. 2025 年 9⽉19-21⽇，随机方法与智能计算研讨会，上海Title: Data-driven Model Reduction for Parameter-Dependent MatrixEquations via Operator Inference

Abstract: This work develops a non-intrusive, data-driven surrogate modeling framework based on Operator Inference (OpInf) for rapidly solving parameter-dependent matrix equations in many-query settings. Motivated by the requirements of the OpInf methodology, we reformulate the matrix equations into a structured representation that explicitly shows the parameter dependence in polynomial form. This reformulation is 

crucial for efficient model reduction. This approach constructs reduced-order models via regression on solution snapshots, bypassing the need for expensive full-order operators and thus overcoming the primary bottlenecks of intrusive methods in high-dimensional contexts. Numerical experiments confirm their accuracy and computational efficiency, demonstrating that our work is a scalable and practical solution for parameter-dependent matrix equations.
