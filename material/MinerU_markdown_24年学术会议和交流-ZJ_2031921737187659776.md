1. 2024 年 3⽉29-31⽇，Opportunities and Challenges inNumerical Algebra Academic Seminar，珠海

Title: Data-Driven Optimal Iterative Parameter Prediction and its Applications 

Abstract: Matrix splitting iterative methods with parameters play a crucial role in solving linear systems. How to choose optimal splitting parameters is a key problem. In this talk, we propose a data-driven approach for predicting optimal iterative parameters: multi-task kernel learning Gaussian regression prediction (GPR) method. We develop the generalized alternating direction implicit (GADI) framework with optimal parameters, successfully integrating it as a smoother in algebraic multigrid methods to solve linear systems. Moreover, we accelerate GPR using mixed precision strategy and evaluate the predicted results with statistical indicators. Further, we have successfully applied GPR to (time-dependent) linear algebraic systems (elliptic equations, Poisson equations, convection-diffusion equations, Helmholtz equations) and linear matrix equations (Sylvester equations). Numerical results illustrate our methods can save an enormous amount of time in selecting the relatively optimal splitting parameters compared with the exists methods. When the system size exceeds hundreds of thousands, the acceleration ratio of the GADI framework can reach hundreds to thousands of times.

# 5. 2024 年 5 月 10-12 日，江苏师大，The 4th InternationalConference on Quaternion Matrix Computations withApplications

Title: Numerical Method and Preconditioning of Quasiperiodic Elliptic Equations 

Abstract: In this study, our primary objective is to address the challenge of efficiently solving elliptic equations with quasiperiodic coefficients from a numerical algebra perspective. To achieve accurate and efficient computations, we introduce the projection method, which enables the embedding of quasiperiodic systems into higher-dimensional periodic systems. To further enhance computational efficiency and stability, we propose a compressed storage strategy for the stiffness matrix, reducing memory requirements while maintaining accuracy. Most importantly, we design and implement a diagonal preconditioner to efficiently solve the resulting high-dimensional linear system by significantly reducing the condition number of the stiffness matrix. These combined techniques contribute significantly to the computational effectiveness of our proposed approach. We demonstrate the effectiveness and accuracy of our approach through a series of numerical examples and apply our method to achieve highly accurate computation of the homogenized coefficients for a quasiperiodic multiscale elliptic equation.

13. 2024 年 10⽉24-27⽇，CSIAM2024，TM51：代数特征值问题的算法与应用, 南京

Title: Numerical method and preconditioning of quasiperiodic Schrödinger eigenproblems 

Abstract: In quantum systems, the localization of waves is closely related to the electrical structure’s disorder. The quasiperiodic Schrödinger eigenproblem is a typical mathematical model that describes this phenomenon. However, due to quasiperiodic potentials are space- filling order without decay or translation invariance, the condition numbers of quasiperiodic Schrödinger operators very poor. In this report, I will introduce a new algorithm, the irrational-window-filtered projection method (IWFPM), which uses the concentrated distribution feature of spectral points to further improve the projection method. Moreover, we also designed a preconditioner that perfectly matches quasiperiodic Schrödinger operators, drastically reducing each condition number from tens of thousands to single digits. We apply IWFPM to one-, two- and three-dimensional quasiperiodic Schrödinger eigenproblems to demonstrate the accuracy and efficiency of our proposed method. Especially when solving localized quantum states, IWFPM shows significant advantages over the projection method.

# 18. 2024 年 12⽉10⽇，南昌大学

题目：Ohta-Kawasaki 方程的两种预处理方法

摘要：二嵌段共聚物是由两个不相容的嵌段通过共价键连接在一起的大分子，建模和数值模拟是研究嵌段共聚物相行为的有效手段。Ohta和Kawasaki在1986年提出了一种研究二嵌段共聚物的有效自由能泛函，即 Ohta-Kawasaki 方程。在本次报告中，我们对质量守恒的Ohta-Kawasaki 方程提出了两种高效的预处理方法，即 Schur 补预处理和 MHSS 预处理，并且给出了相应的谱分析。此外，我们采用牛顿法及其变形形式解决隐式非线性项，并且严格地分析了牛顿迭代法的收敛性。最后，我们通过数值例子验证了理论分析的正确性和预处理矩阵的有效性。
